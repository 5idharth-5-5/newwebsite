ABOUT DEPARTMENT

The MCA Department started functioning in the year 2005 with a sanctioned strength of sixty students The department remains committed towards its goal of producing not only industry ready professionals, but also to provide the right ambience and infrastructure for those interested in pursuing their interest in computers and related fields. Master of Computer Applications programme is a two year course of intensive theory and practical work. It is spread over a period of six semesters which enables the candidates to get familiar with the usage of computer using at least five latest programming languages and a large number of applications. Expert faculty drawn from industry and academia are the catalysts to the learning on campus and facilitate valuable exposure to the learning on campus and provide anintegrated multidisciplinary program with innovative teaching methods to enhance analytic and problem solving skills,personality development module to tap the full potential of the students by improving time and self management communication skills, team spirit, confidence and career building aptitude.

Vision of the Department

To become a Centre of excellence in research, education, development and consultancy in the field of Computer Applications.

Mission of the Department

M1: Provide a strong theoretical and practical competence in computer application.
M2: Provide students with necessary skills to become successful professionals, responsible citizens and life-long learners.
M3: Develop technical solutions in the field of Information Technology for the society and Industry.

Program Educational Objective

PEO 1: Establish career in software industry, academia or launch startup.
PEO 2: Pursue continuous learning through higher studies and research in the field of Computer Applications and other allied streams.
PEO 3: Establish themselves as technical solution architects by the application of Information Technology for the benefit of common man and society.
